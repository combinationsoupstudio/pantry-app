=== Shakshuka
id: shakshuka
serves: 4
minutes: 30
tags: breakfast, eggs, vegetarian
credit: original
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 red capsicum, sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed | garlic
- 2 tsp ground cumin | cumin
- 2 tsp smoked paprika | paprika
- 800 g tinned tomatoes | tinned tomatoes; diced tomatoes; crushed tomatoes
- 6 eggs
- 60 g feta, crumbled
- coriander leaves, to serve | coriander; parsley
- crusty bread, to serve | bread; sourdough
~ salt
> Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Cook the onion and capsicum for 8 minutes, until soft and sweet.
> Add the garlic, cumin and paprika and stir for 1 minute, until it smells toasty.
> Tip in the tomatoes, season with salt, and simmer for 10 minutes until thick enough to hold a dip made with a spoon.
> Make 6 dips in the sauce and crack an egg into each. Cover and cook on low for 6 to 8 minutes, until the whites are set and the yolks still soft.
> Scatter over the feta and coriander. Serve straight from the pan with bread for mopping.

=== Corn fritters with avocado smash

- 1/2 cup milk

- 50 g tasty cheese, grated | cheese; tasty cheese
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil | vegetable oil; canola oil; olive oil; oil
-- Smash
- 2 avocados | avocado
- 1 lemon | lemon; lime

~ salt
~ black pepper

=== Banana oat pancakes
id: banana-oat-pancakes
serves: 4
minutes: 25
tags: breakfast, kids, vegetarian
credit: original
- 2 ripe bananas | bananas; banana
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup self-raising flour | self-raising flour; self raising flour; flour
- 1/2 cup rolled oats | oats; rolled oats
- 1 cup milk
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon | cinnamon
- 20 g butter, for the pan | butter
- maple syrup, to serve | maple syrup; honey
> Mash the bananas in a bowl until almost smooth. Whisk in the eggs and milk.
> Stir in the flour, oats and cinnamon until just combined. Rest for 5 minutes so the oats soften.
> Melt a little butter in a non-stick pan over medium-low heat. Pour in quarter-cup amounts.
> Cook until bubbles cover the top, about 2 minutes, then flip and cook 1 minute more.
> Serve warm with maple syrup.
note: Low heat matters: the banana browns fast.

=== Beef and broccoli stir-fry
id: beef-broccoli
serves: 4
minutes: 25
tags: dinner, beef, quick
credit: original
- 500 g rump steak, thinly sliced | rump steak; beef strips; steak; beef
- 1 head broccoli, cut into florets | broccoli
- 1/4 cup oyster sauce
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp brown sugar | brown sugar; sugar
- 2 tsp cornflour
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed | garlic
- 2 tsp grated ginger | ginger
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil | vegetable oil; canola oil; oil
- 1 1/2 cups rice | rice; jasmine rice
> Cook the rice.
> Toss the beef with 1 teaspoon of the cornflour and a splash of the soy.
> Mix the oyster sauce, the rest of the soy, sugar, the rest of the cornflour and 1/2 cup water.

=== Thai basil pork mince
id: thai-basil-pork
serves: 4
minutes: 20
tags: dinner, pork, quick, spicy
credit: original
- 500 g pork mince | pork mince; mince
- 4 garlic cloves, chopped | garlic
- 2 red chillies, chopped | chilli; chillies
- 1 red capsicum, sliced
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp fish sauce
- 2 tsp brown sugar | brown sugar; sugar
- 1 bunch Thai basil | thai basil; basil
- 4 eggs
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil | vegetable oil; canola oil; oil
- 1 1/2 cups rice | rice; jasmine rice
> Cook the rice.
> Pound or finely chop the garlic and chilli together.

=== Butter chicken
id: butter-chicken
serves: 6
minutes: 50
tags: dinner, chicken, curry, Indian
credit: original
-- Marinade
- 1 kg chicken thigh fillets, cut into chunks | chicken thighs; chicken thigh; chicken
- 1/2 cup Greek yoghurt | yoghurt; greek yoghurt
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground turmeric | turmeric
-- Sauce
- 50 g butter
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed | garlic
- 1 tbsp grated ginger | ginger
- 2 tsp ground cumin | cumin
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp chilli powder | chilli powder; chilli flakes
- 400 g passata | passata; tinned tomatoes
- 3/4 cup thickened cream | cream; thickened cream
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2 cups basmati rice | basmati rice; rice
~ salt
> Mix the chicken with the yoghurt, garam masala and turmeric. Leave for 15 minutes, or overnight in the fridge.
> Cook the rice.
> Grill or pan-fry the chicken over high heat in batches until charred at the edges. It does not need to be cooked through yet.
> Melt the butter in a large pan over medium heat. Cook the onion for 8 minutes until soft and golden.
> Add the garlic, ginger, cumin, coriander and chilli powder and stir for 1 minute.
> Pour in the passata and simmer for 10 minutes. Blend smooth if you like.
> Stir in the cream, sugar and chicken. Simmer 10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through. Season with salt and serve with the rice.

~ salt
~ black pepper

=== Chicken and leek pie

=== Slow-cooked beef ragu
id: slow-beef-ragu
serves: 6
minutes: 210
tags: dinner, beef, Italian, slow cooker
credit: original
- 1 kg chuck steak, in 4 big pieces | chuck steak; gravy beef; beef
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 carrot, finely chopped
- 2 celery sticks, finely chopped | celery
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed | garlic
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 cup red wine | red wine; wine
- 800 g tinned tomatoes | tinned tomatoes; diced tomatoes; crushed tomatoes
- 1 cup beef stock | beef stock; stock
- 2 bay leaves
- 500 g pappardelle | pappardelle; pasta; fettuccine
- parmesan, to serve | parmesan; cheese
~ salt
~ black pepper
> Season the beef well. Brown it all over in the oil in a heavy pot over high heat, then set aside.
> Lower to medium. Cook the onion, carrot and celery for 10 minutes until soft.
> Add the garlic and tomato paste for 2 minutes, then the wine. Bubble for 2 minutes.
> Add the tomatoes, stock, bay leaves and beef. Cover and simmer very gently for 3 hours (or 8 hours on low in a slow cooker) until the beef falls apart.
> Shred the beef into the sauce with two forks and season.
> Cook the pasta, toss with the ragu and a splash of pasta water, and serve with parmesan.
note: Freezes well for 3 months.

=== Moroccan lamb with couscous
id: moroccan-lamb-couscous
serves: 4
minutes: 35
tags: dinner, lamb, Moroccan
credit: original
- 600 g lamb backstrap or leg steaks | lamb backstrap; lamb steaks; lamb
- 2 tsp ground cumin | cumin
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon | cinnamon
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 1/2 cups couscous | couscous
- 1 1/2 cups chicken stock | chicken stock; stock
- 400 g tinned chickpeas, drained | chickpeas
- 1/2 cup dried apricots, chopped | dried apricots; apricots
- 1/3 cup slivered almonds | almonds
- 1 lemon
- 1/2 cup Greek yoghurt | yoghurt; greek yoghurt
- 1/2 cup chopped mint | mint
~ salt
> Rub the lamb with the spices, half the oil and salt. Leave 10 minutes.
> Bring the stock to the boil, pour over the couscous, cover and leave 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork.
> Warm the chickpeas, apricots and almonds in the rest of the oil for 3 minutes. Stir through the couscous with lemon zest, juice and mint.
> Cook the lamb in a hot pan 3 minutes a side for medium. Rest 5 minutes, then slice.
> Serve the lamb on the couscous with a spoon of yoghurt.

=== Prawn and chorizo paella
id: prawn-chorizo-paella
serves: 6
minutes: 50
tags: dinner, seafood, Spanish, one pot
credit: original
- 2 chorizo sausages, sliced | chorizo
- 500 g raw prawns, peeled | prawns; raw prawns
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 red capsicum, sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed | garlic
- 2 tsp smoked paprika | paprika
- 1 pinch saffron threads | saffron
- 1 1/2 cups paella rice | paella rice; arborio rice; rice
- 400 g tinned tomatoes | tinned tomatoes; diced tomatoes
- 1 litre chicken stock | chicken stock; stock
- 1 cup frozen peas | frozen peas; peas
- 1 lemon
~ salt
> Soak the saffron in the warm stock.
> Fry the chorizo in the oil in a large wide pan until crisp. Take it out.
> Cook the onion and capsicum for 6 minutes. Add the garlic, paprika and rice and stir for 1 minute.
> Add the tomatoes, stock and chorizo. Shake the pan level and simmer without stirring for 18 minutes.
> Press the prawns and peas into the top. Cook 5 minutes more, until the prawns are pink and the bottom is lightly crusted.
> Rest 5 minutes off the heat under a tea towel. Serve with lemon wedges.
