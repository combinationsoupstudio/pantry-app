=== Smoko burritos
id: smoko-burritos
serves: 6
minutes: 55
tags: dinner, beef, Mexican, signature
credit: original
-- Smoko sauce
- 4 tomatoes, halved | tomatoes; tomato
- 1 onion, quartered | onion; brown onion
- 4 garlic cloves, skin on | garlic
- 1 jalapeno | jalapeno; green chilli; chilli
- 2 chipotles in adobo, plus 1 tbsp of the sauce | chipotle in adobo; chipotles
- 1 lime | lime; lemon
- 1 tsp honey | honey
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil | vegetable oil; canola oil; oil
-- Seasoning
- 2 tsp smoked paprika | smoked paprika; paprika
- 2 tsp ground cumin | cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano | oregano
- 1 tsp garlic powder | garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder | onion powder
- 1 tsp cocoa powder | cocoa
- 2 tsp brown sugar | brown sugar; sugar
- 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon | cinnamon
-- Meat
- 600 g beef mince, not lean | beef mince; mince
- 1 chorizo sausage, skin off | chorizo
- 1 tbsp tomato paste | tomato paste
- 1 lime | lime; lemon
-- To fill
- 6 large tortillas | tortillas; wraps
- 1 cup rice | rice
- 400 g tinned black beans, drained | black beans; beans
- 150 g tasty cheese, grated | tasty cheese; cheese
- 1/2 cup sour cream | sour cream
- 1 avocado | avocado
- 2 cups shredded cos lettuce | cos lettuce; lettuce
- coriander, to serve | coriander
~ salt
~ black pepper
> Cook the rice. Stir in the zest of one lime and a pinch of salt.
> Sauce: heat a heavy dry pan or grill on high. Char the tomatoes cut side down, the onion, garlic and jalapeno for 10 to 12 minutes, turning, until blackened in patches.
> Peel the garlic. Blend everything charred with the chipotles, adobo, juice of 1 lime, honey and 1/2 tsp salt until smooth.
> Heat the oil in a saucepan until shimmering. Pour in the sauce (it spits) and simmer 8 minutes until darker and thick. This frying is what makes it.
> Mix the seasoning with 1 1/2 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp pepper.
> Meat: heat a large frying pan on high until smoking. Press the mince into one flat layer and leave it alone for 4 minutes, until a dark crust forms. Flip in pieces and break it up.
> Add the chorizo and cook 3 minutes. Push it all to one side, add the tomato paste and seasoning to the fat, and fry 1 minute until it darkens.
> Stir it through, add 1/2 cup Smoko sauce and 1/4 cup water, and cook 4 minutes until sticky. Squeeze over the second lime.
> Warm the beans. Fill each tortilla: rice, beans, meat, cheese, a spoon of sauce, lettuce, avocado, sour cream. Fold in the sides and roll tight.
> Toast seam side down in a dry pan for 2 minutes a side. Serve the rest of the sauce for dipping.
note: Double the Smoko sauce: it keeps a week in the fridge and freezes.
note: Too hot for kids? One chipotle and no jalapeno.

=== Lamb biryani
id: lamb-biryani
serves: 8
minutes: 120
tags: dinner, lamb, rice, Indian, weekend
credit: qld-hnc
-- Fried onion paste
- 6 onions, finely sliced | onions; onion; brown onion
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil | vegetable oil; canola oil; oil
- 1 tbsp natural yoghurt | natural yoghurt; yoghurt
- 1 tsp ground turmeric | turmeric
-- Marinade
- 1 cup natural yoghurt | natural yoghurt; yoghurt
- 1 tbsp grated ginger | ginger
- 5 garlic cloves, crushed | garlic
- 2 tbsp biryani masala | biryani masala; garam masala
- 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder | kashmiri chilli; chilli powder; paprika
- 2 long red chillies, finely chopped | long red chillies; red chilli; chilli
- 1 lemon | lemon
- 1/2 bunch coriander, chopped | coriander
- 1/2 bunch mint, chopped | mint
-- Meat
- 800 g lamb leg or shoulder, cut in 2 cm pieces | diced lamb; lamb
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil | vegetable oil; canola oil; oil
-- Rice
- 2 1/2 cups basmati rice | basmati rice; rice
- 2 bay leaves | bay leaves
- 1 cinnamon stick | cinnamon stick; cinnamon
- 1 star anise | star anise
- 6 green cardamom pods | cardamom pods; cardamom
- 6 whole cloves | cloves
- 1 tsp cumin seeds | cumin seeds; cumin
-- Layers
- 1/4 cup milk | milk
- 1 big pinch saffron | saffron
- 1 tbsp ghee | ghee; butter
- 1/2 bunch coriander, chopped | coriander
- 1/2 bunch mint, chopped | mint
- 1/2 cup roasted cashews | cashews
~ salt
~ black pepper
> Fry the onion in the oil over medium heat, turning often, for about 15 minutes until deep golden. Keep back 1/2 cup for the layers and blend the rest with the yoghurt and turmeric into a paste.
> Mix the marinade in a glass or ceramic bowl with the juice of the lemon, 1/2 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp pepper. Stir in the onion paste and the lamb. Cover and refrigerate at least 3 hours, or overnight.
> Rinse the rice until the water runs clear, soak it in cold water for 30 minutes, then drain.
> Heat the oil in a large pan over medium-high heat and cook the lamb with all its marinade for 15 to 20 minutes, stirring often, until thick.
> Warm the milk until nearly boiling and drop in the saffron.
> Bring a big pot of salted water to the boil with the bay leaves and whole spices. Add the rice, boil 5 minutes only, then drain.
> Melt the ghee in a heavy pot. Layer a third of the rice, half the lamb, some herbs, some fried onion and 1 tbsp saffron milk. Repeat, then finish with the last of the rice and saffron milk.
> Heat until you see steam, then turn to low. Seal the pot with foil and the lid and cook 20 to 25 minutes.
> Rest 10 minutes off the heat. Uncover and scatter with cashews, the rest of the fried onion and herbs.
note: Serve with raita and a cucumber tomato salad.
note: No biryani masala? Use garam masala with an extra pinch of cardamom.

=== Ghormeh sabzi
id: ghormeh-sabzi
serves: 8
minutes: 140
tags: dinner, beef, Persian, slow cook
credit: qld-hnc
- 3 tbsp olive oil | olive oil; oil
- 1 kg beef chuck steak, trimmed and diced | chuck steak; beef chuck; stewing beef; beef
- 1 onion, sliced | onion; brown onion
- 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped | garlic
- 1 tbsp tomato paste | tomato paste
- 2 tsp ground turmeric | turmeric
- 1 tsp ground cumin | cumin
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon | cinnamon
- 2 tbsp dried fenugreek leaves, if you have them | dried fenugreek; fenugreek
- 3 dried limes, if you have them | dried limes
- 1 tsp stock powder | stock powder; beef stock powder
- 400 g tinned red kidney beans, drained | red kidney beans; kidney beans
- 1 lemon | lemon
- 3 cups baby spinach | baby spinach; spinach
- 1 big handful parsley, dill or coriander | parsley; dill; coriander
- 4 spring onions, green parts | spring onions
~ salt
~ black pepper
> Heat the oven to 160°C. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a flameproof, ovenproof pot over medium-high heat.
> Brown the beef in two batches until dark on all sides, setting it aside as you go.
> Add the rest of the oil and the onion and cook 3 minutes. Stir in the garlic, tomato paste and spices (and the fenugreek and dried limes, pierced, if using) for 1 minute.
> Return the beef, add 1 1/2 cups water, the stock powder, 1/2 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp pepper. Bring to the boil.
> Cover and bake 1 1/2 hours.
> Chop the spinach, herbs and spring onions. Stir them in with the beans and the juice of the lemon, and bake 30 minutes more.
note: Serve with rice and a crisp salad.
note: Frying the herbs in a little oil for 5 minutes before they go in deepens the flavour.

=== Ashak (herb dumplings with tomato lentil sauce)
id: ashak
serves: 6
minutes: 90
tags: dinner, vegetarian, Afghan, weekend
credit: qld-hnc
-- Dough
- 500 g plain flour | plain flour; flour
- 3 tbsp vegetable oil | vegetable oil; canola oil; oil
-- Tomato lentil sauce
- 1/2 cup split yellow lentils | yellow lentils; split lentils; lentils
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil | vegetable oil; canola oil; oil
- 1 onion, chopped | onion; brown onion
- 4 garlic cloves, finely chopped | garlic
- 2 tbsp tomato paste | tomato paste
- 3 tomatoes, grated | tomatoes; tomato
- 1 tsp ground turmeric | turmeric
- 2 tsp char masala or garam masala | garam masala; char masala
-- Filling
- 3 bunches garlic chives, chopped | garlic chives; chives
- 1/2 bunch coriander, chopped | coriander
- 1 handful mint, chopped | mint
- 1/2 tsp chilli powder | chilli powder
-- Yoghurt sauce
- 2 cups natural yoghurt | natural yoghurt; yoghurt
- 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped | garlic
-- To serve
- 2 tsp dried mint | dried mint; mint
~ salt
~ black pepper
> Mix the flour, oil and 2 tsp salt with 1/2 cup water, adding a little more water until it comes together. Knead 8 to 10 minutes until soft and stretchy. Cut into 10 balls and rest them, covered, 30 minutes.
> Simmer the lentils in plenty of water for 20 minutes until tender, then drain.
> Fry the onion and garlic in the oil for 3 to 4 minutes. Add the tomato paste, turmeric, masala, salt and pepper and cook 2 minutes. Add the tomatoes and lentils and cook gently 10 to 15 minutes.
> Mix the chives, herbs, chilli powder, 1 tsp salt and 1 tsp oil for the filling.
> Roll one dough ball very thin and cut circles about the length of your hand. Put 1 to 2 tsp filling in each, fold over and press to seal, then bring the ends together into a flower. Repeat.
> Steam the dumplings 8 to 10 minutes.
> Mix the yoghurt, garlic and 1 tsp salt. Spread half on a big platter, add the dumplings, spoon over the lentil sauce and the rest of the yoghurt. Finish with fresh herbs and dried mint.
note: Dumplings freeze well uncooked: steam from frozen for 12 minutes.

=== Tepsi baytinijan (eggplant and kofta bake)
id: tepsi-baytinijan
serves: 8
minutes: 120
tags: dinner, beef, lamb, Iraqi, bake, weekend
credit: qld-hnc
-- Kofta
- 1 onion | onion; brown onion
- 2 garlic cloves | garlic
- 2 large handfuls parsley | parsley
- 350 g beef mince | beef mince; mince
- 350 g lamb mince | lamb mince; mince
- 1 tbsp baharat | baharat; seven spice; allspice
- 2 tbsp plain flour | plain flour; flour
-- Tomato sauce
- 1 tbsp olive oil | olive oil; oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped | onion; brown onion
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed | garlic
- 1 tbsp baharat | baharat; seven spice; allspice
- 2 tsp ground turmeric | turmeric
- 1 tsp paprika | paprika
- 1 tbsp tomato paste | tomato paste
- 800 g tomato puree | tomato puree; passata
- 1 tsp dried mint (optional) | dried mint
-- Vegetables
- 2 large eggplants, in 1.5 cm slices | eggplants; eggplant
- 3 potatoes | potatoes; potato
- 1 red capsicum, in strips | red capsicum; capsicum
- 2 onions, in 1 cm rings | onions; onion
- oil spray | oil spray; oil
~ salt
~ black pepper
~ sugar
> Blitz the onion, garlic and parsley in a food processor. Add both minces, baharat, flour, 1 tsp salt and pepper and pulse to a paste. Chill 30 minutes.
> Sauce: soften the onion in the oil 2 to 3 minutes. Add the garlic, spices and tomato paste for 1 minute, then the puree and a pinch of sugar. Cook on low 15 minutes until dark, add 1 cup water and simmer 10 to 15 minutes more until glossy.
> Boil the potatoes whole for 6 to 8 minutes, cool, peel and slice 1 cm thick.
> Shape the kofta into flat fingers. Brown them 1 minute a side in a hot oiled pan.
> In the same pan on high, spray the eggplant with oil and brown it 2 to 3 minutes a side, in batches. Heat the oven to 180°C.
> Oil a big baking dish. Lay the potato, onion rings, capsicum and eggplant in alternating rows, sit the kofta on top and pour over the sauce.
> Cover with foil and bake 1 hour. Serve with basmati rice.

=== Bakso (meatball noodle soup)
id: bakso
serves: 6
minutes: 40
tags: dinner, soup, beef, Indonesian, noodles
credit: qld-hnc
-- Meatballs
- 500 g beef mince | beef mince; mince
- 1/2 tsp baking powder | baking powder
- 1 egg | eggs; egg
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed | garlic
- 1 tsp beef stock powder | beef stock powder; stock powder
- 1 tbsp fried shallots, crushed | fried shallots
- 1 handful coriander, very finely chopped (optional) | coriander
- 1/2 cup tapioca flour or cornflour | tapioca flour; cornflour
-- Soup
- 1 bunch spring onions | spring onions
- 2 tsp white peppercorns | white pepper; peppercorns
- 5 garlic cloves | garlic
- 1 tbsp grated ginger | ginger
- 1 tsp ground nutmeg | nutmeg
- 3 celery sticks, sliced | celery
- 1 cinnamon stick | cinnamon stick; cinnamon
- 1 star anise (optional) | star anise
-- To serve
- 400 g rice or egg noodles | noodles; egg noodles; rice noodles
- 3 bunches bok choy, sliced | bok choy
- 200 g bean sprouts | bean sprouts
- kecap manis | kecap manis; sweet soy sauce
- chilli sauce | chilli sauce
- fried shallots, to top | fried shallots
~ salt
~ black pepper
> Bring a large pot of water to the boil.
> Mix the mince, baking powder, egg, garlic, stock powder, 1/2 tsp pepper, fried shallots and coriander. Work in the flour and about 1/3 cup iced water a little at a time until it holds together.
> Squeeze the mixture up between thumb and finger and scoop off balls with a spoon. Boil 5 to 7 minutes, lift out, and keep the water.
> Pound the white parts of the spring onions, peppercorns, garlic, ginger and nutmeg to a paste. Stir into the meatball water with the celery, cinnamon, star anise and 1 to 2 tsp salt. Simmer 5 minutes.
> Return the meatballs and simmer 5 minutes more.
> Cook the noodles to the packet, and blanch the bok choy 1 to 2 minutes.
> Fill bowls with noodles, bok choy and sprouts, add kecap manis and chilli to taste, then ladle over the soup and meatballs. Top with fried shallots and the spring onion greens.

=== Shorbat addas (lentil soup)
id: shorbat-addas
serves: 6
minutes: 35
tags: lunch, dinner, soup, vegetarian, vegan, Middle Eastern, budget
credit: qld-hnc
- 1 onion | onion; brown onion
- 2 carrots | carrots; carrot
- 2 garlic cloves | garlic
- 1 tbsp olive oil | olive oil; oil
- 1 tbsp tomato paste | tomato paste
- 1 tsp ground cumin | cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander | ground coriander
- 1 tsp ground turmeric | turmeric
- 1 tbsp stock powder | stock powder; vegetable stock powder
- 1 1/2 cups red lentils | red lentils; lentils
- 1 lemon | lemon
- 1 handful parsley or coriander | parsley; coriander
~ salt
~ black pepper
> Finely chop the onion, carrots and garlic. Rinse the lentils until the water runs clear.
> Cook the onion and carrot in the oil over medium-high heat for 3 minutes. Add the garlic, tomato paste and spices for 1 minute.

=== Borani banjan (eggplant with tomato and yoghurt)
id: borani-banjan
serves: 6
minutes: 30
tags: dinner, side, vegetarian, Afghan
credit: qld-hnc
- 2 eggplants | eggplants; eggplant
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil | vegetable oil; canola oil; oil
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed | garlic
- 1 tbsp tomato paste | tomato paste
- 400 g tinned diced tomatoes | diced tomatoes; tinned tomatoes
- 1 tsp ground turmeric | turmeric
- 1/2 tsp chilli flakes | chilli flakes; chilli
- 1 cup natural yoghurt | natural yoghurt; yoghurt
- 1 garlic clove, crushed | garlic
- 2 tsp dried or fresh mint | mint; dried mint
- wholemeal flatbread, to serve | flatbread
~ salt
~ black pepper

=== Maraq digaag (chicken and lentil soup)

credit: qld-hnc
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil | vegetable oil; canola oil; oil
- 2 onions, chopped | onions; onion
- 6 garlic cloves, finely chopped | garlic
- 1 kg chicken legs or drumsticks | chicken drumsticks; chicken legs; chicken
- 3 tsp xawaash spice or baharat | xawaash; baharat; allspice
- 3 tsp stock powder | stock powder; chicken stock powder

=== Bacon and egg rolls with tomato relish

=== Pavlova
id: pavlova

tags: dessert, sweet, Christmas, gluten free, Australian

- 6 egg whites, at room temperature | eggs
- 1 1/2 cups caster sugar | caster sugar; sugar
- 2 tsp cornflour | cornflour
- 1 tsp white vinegar | white vinegar; vinegar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract | vanilla extract; vanilla
- 300 ml thickened cream | thickened cream; cream
- 4 passionfruit | passionfruit
- 250 g strawberries | strawberries; berries
- 2 kiwifruit or 1 mango | kiwifruit; mango
